Make the AI judge feel held. Your program writes one text (a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it), an AI judge compares the texts two at a time, and the 20 best win rates share $50. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the AI judge feel held” bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes one short, original text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet scene (moonlight, a cat, a settling house) meant to slow the reader down and leave them held, with nothing to prove. Deterministic, no network, no randomness.
